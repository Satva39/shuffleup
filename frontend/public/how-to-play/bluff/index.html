How to Play Bluff (Cheat) Online

Bluff, sometimes called Cheat, is a social deduction card game where players make rank claims about face-down cards. Opponents decide whether to accept the claim or challenge it during the challenge window.

Objective

Get rid of your cards while choosing when to make truthful claims and when to bluff. Avoid taking the full face-down pile after a failed challenge.

How to play Bluff (Cheat): step by step

  1. The server deals a standard 52-card deck. The required claim rank begins at Ace.
  2. On your turn, play one to four cards face-down and claim that they match the currently required rank. Opponents cannot see the normal card identities.
  3. The required rank advances through A, 2, 3 and so on through K, then returns to A.
  4. During the challenge window, an opponent can challenge the claim. The server reveals and evaluates the play, and the penalty pile is awarded according to the actual result.
  5. Play continues until a player’s final empty-hand claim survives the required resolution. The game does not use a numeric score; taking the pile is the key penalty.

Tips for a new player

  • Track the rank currently required for a claim.
  • Consider what previous plays suggest, while remembering face-down cards remain hidden until a challenge.
  • Do not assume a final empty hand wins until its challenge phase has resolved.

Frequently asked questions

How many cards can be claimed in one play?

The current rules allow one to four face-down cards per claim.

Does Bluff have a score total?

No separate numeric score is used; the pile penalty and final claim determine the result.
