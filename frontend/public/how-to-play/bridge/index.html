SHUFFLEUP GAME GUIDE

How to Play Bridge Online

Bridge is a partnership trick-taking card game. ShuffleUp uses four seats—North, East, South and West—with North/South on one team and East/West on the other.

Objective

Win the number of tricks required by your final contract and earn the associated contract score.

How to play Bridge: step by step

  1. The server deals all 52 cards, giving each of the four players 13 cards.
  2. Players take part in an auction to determine the contract, including its level and trump suit or No Trump.
  3. The contract may involve doubling or redoubling when the auction permits it. The final contract determines the declarer side’s target.
  4. The opening lead begins trick play, and the dummy hand is revealed as required by the game flow.
  5. Play through 13 tricks. Trump beats a non-trump card; in No Trump, the highest card of the led suit wins the trick. The server calculates the contract result and score.

Tips for a new player

  • Communicate through legal bids rather than assuming your partner can see your cards.
  • Track the contract and remaining trump cards as tricks progress.
  • Remember the partnership seating: North/South against East/West.

Frequently asked questions

How many players are needed for Bridge?

Four players, arranged in two partnerships.

Does ShuffleUp have a fixed match target for Bridge?

The current implementation records deal results but does not define one global match target.

Rules note: This guide follows the version currently implemented on ShuffleUp. Some traditional card games have regional variants, and local rules may differ. The game result and valid actions inside ShuffleUp are determined by its game server.
