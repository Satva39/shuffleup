How to Play Indian Rummy Online

Indian Rummy is a hand-building card game. In ShuffleUp, each player starts with 13 cards and tries to form valid groups before making a declaration. A valid declaration must satisfy the implementation’s sequence requirements.

Objective

Arrange the 13 cards into valid groups, including at least one pure sequence and at least two sequences in total, then declare successfully.

How to play Indian Rummy: step by step

  1. The game uses two standard 52-card decks plus two printed Jokers. Each player receives 13 cards, and one card starts the discard pile.
  2. A turn begins by drawing one card from either the closed deck or the top of the discard pile.
  3. Arrange your hand into sequences and sets. A sequence uses consecutive ranks of one suit; a set uses the same rank in different suits.
  4. The selected wild-joker rank and the printed Jokers can act as jokers when the server validates groups. A pure sequence does not use a joker as a substitute.
  5. After drawing, discard one card. When you believe your hand satisfies the declaration requirements, submit a declaration for server validation.

Tips for a new player

  • Build a pure sequence early because it is a required part of a valid declaration.
  • Keep track of whether your hand contains at least two sequences, not just sets.
  • Avoid holding cards that are difficult to fit into a valid group as the hand develops.

Frequently asked questions

How many cards do you start with?

Each player starts with 13 cards.

What makes a declaration valid?

The 13-card hand must contain at least one pure sequence and at least two sequences in total, according to the current game rules.
