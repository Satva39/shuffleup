How to Play Kachuful Online

Kachuful is a trick-taking card game where every player predicts how many tricks they will win in a round. ShuffleUp implements a variable-round version: hands grow from one card per player up to the largest deal supported by the player count.

Objective

Predict your trick total accurately, then play carefully enough to meet that bid. Your result across the rounds determines the final standings.

How to play Kachuful: step by step

  1. The server deals one card to each player in the first round. Later rounds add one more card per player.
  2. Each player places a private bid for the number of tricks they expect to win. Bids are revealed after everyone has submitted one.
  3. Players take turns playing one card into each trick. Follow the suit led when you can; if you cannot follow suit, another suit can be played.
  4. The round trump rotates in this order: Spades, Diamonds, Clubs, Hearts. The trump is determined by the round rather than selected by a player.
  5. At round end, an exact bid earns 10 plus the bid value. A non-exact bid earns zero for that round. The game continues through its configured rounds.

Tips for a new player

  • Estimate how many tricks your highest cards and trump suit can realistically take.
  • Track which cards in the leading suit have already appeared.
  • Remember that an accurate bid matters more than taking the largest possible number of tricks.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can play Kachuful on ShuffleUp?

The current implementation supports 4 to 10 players.

How is a round scored?

An exact trick prediction scores 10 plus the bid; a miss scores zero for that round.

Rules note: This guide follows the version currently implemented on ShuffleUp. Some traditional card games have regional variants, and local rules may differ. The game result and valid actions inside ShuffleUp are determined by its game server.
