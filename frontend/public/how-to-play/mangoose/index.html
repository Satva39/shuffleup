How to Play Mangoose Online

Mangoose is a card-placement and shedding game with personal closed and open piles, plus four central foundations—one for each suit. The goal is to empty your own cards while making legal placements.

Objective

Reduce your own piles and finish ahead of the other players. The game tracks finishes and continues according to the server’s completion rules.

How to play Mangoose: step by step

  1. The server deals a standard 52-card deck round-robin into players’ closed piles. Four central suit foundations start empty.
  2. On a turn, reveal or use an available card from the relevant pile and choose a legal destination.
  3. A suit’s central foundation begins with its Ace. Later plays to that foundation must be exactly one rank above or below its current top card of the same suit.
  4. An opponent’s open pile accepts only the immediately ascending rank; suit does not determine that placement.
  5. The game also includes a Mongoose call mechanic with a short response window and a server-resolved penalty when its conditions are met. Follow the current on-screen prompt for that action.

Tips for a new player

  • Check the four center foundations before choosing where to play.
  • Remember that center foundations require same-suit adjacency, while opponent open piles use an ascending-rank rule.
  • Use legal moves to reduce your own remaining piles instead of creating avoidable penalties.

Frequently asked questions

How many players can join Mangoose?

ShuffleUp supports 2 to 12 players.

Does Mangoose use points?

The current implementation tracks finishes and remaining card counts rather than a separate numeric score.
