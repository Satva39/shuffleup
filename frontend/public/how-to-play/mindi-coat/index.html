How to Play Mindi Coat Online

Mindi Coat is a four-player partnership trick-taking game. ShuffleUp includes a hidden Hukum card that can later reveal the trump suit, and hand results depend on captured Tens and tricks.

Objective

Help your team win hands by capturing the four Tens and, when required, taking more tricks. The team that reaches five hand points wins the match.

How to play Mindi Coat: step by step

  1. Four players form North/South and East/West partnerships. Each player receives 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.
  2. The player designated by the hand flow selects a Hukum card, which is held hidden outside their normal hand at first.
  3. Players play 13 tricks and must follow the led suit when able. The trick winner leads next.
  4. A player may open Hukum only when they cannot follow suit, during an existing trick, and not if they were the player who originally hid it. When revealed, the card returns to its owner and its suit becomes trump.
  5. The team with more captured Tens wins the hand. If Tens are tied, the team with more tricks wins. A normal hand win scores one point; taking all four Tens (a Coat) scores two. First team to five points wins.

Tips for a new player

  • Count Tens and monitor the trick score as well as ordinary trick wins.
  • Remember the conditions for opening the hidden Hukum card.
  • Coordinate your lead and follow-suit choices with the partnership’s likely winners.

Frequently asked questions

What does Hukum do?

It is a hidden card whose suit becomes trump when legally revealed.

What is a Coat?

Taking all four Tens in a hand scores two points in the current rules.
