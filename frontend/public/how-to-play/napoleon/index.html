SHUFFLEUP GAME GUIDE

How to Play Napoleon Online

ShuffleUp implements a specific five-player Japanese Napoleon-style trick-taking variant. Its bidding, blind, called partner card and special card ranks are defined by the app’s rules and may differ from other regional versions.

Objective

Win the bidding contract and collect enough scoring cards with your team to satisfy the contract.

How to play Napoleon: step by step

  1. The server deals ten cards to each player and places two cards in the blind.
  2. Players bid for the contract. The winning bidder takes the blind and discards two cards according to the game flow.
  3. The bidder calls a partner card, which determines the teammate for that hand under this variant.
  4. Players then play a series of tricks. Follow the leading suit whenever able. Trump and special ranks follow ShuffleUp’s Napoleon rules; these include Mighty (Ace of Spades), trump Jack, a same-color sub-Jack and Same Two behavior after the first trick.
  5. Scoring cards are A, K, Q, J and 10. At the end of the hand, the contract side is checked against the bid and scores are updated. The match runs for eight rounds.

Tips for a new player

  • Understand the contract target and scoring cards before bidding.
  • Remember the called partner card and reassess which side you are on once it is identified.
  • Follow suit when possible and learn the special-rank order before relying on ordinary rank comparisons.

Frequently asked questions

How many players are required?

Exactly five players are supported in ShuffleUp’s current Napoleon variant.

Are all Napoleon variants identical?

No. This guide follows ShuffleUp’s implemented rules; regional versions can differ.
