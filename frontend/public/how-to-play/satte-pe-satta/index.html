How to Play Satte Pe Satta Online

Satte Pe Satta is a sequence-building card game where players extend four suit rows around the sevens. ShuffleUp starts with the 7 of Hearts already placed and uses penalty points based on cards left in players’ hands.

Objective

Empty your hand before the other players. Avoid accumulating penalty points from cards left behind at the end of a round.

How to play Satte Pe Satta: step by step

  1. The game uses a standard 52-card deck and starts automatically with the 7 of Hearts on the table.
  2. Each suit has its own row. Once a suit row opens with its seven, only the immediately lower or immediately higher rank can be appended to that row.
  3. On your turn, place any legal adjacent card. You may pass only when you have no legal move.
  4. The first player to empty their hand wins the round and receives zero penalty points for that round.
  5. Other players add the point values of cards remaining in their hands to their cumulative penalty totals. The game completes when the target threshold of 100 is reached, and the stored winner follows the engine’s ranking.

Tips for a new player

  • Check all four suit rows before deciding whether you can play.
  • An adjacent rank may open new options for later turns, so think about which cards you hold.
  • Avoid passing if you have any legal move; the current rules permit passing only when blocked.

Frequently asked questions

Which card starts the game?

The 7 of Hearts is automatically placed before the first player turn.

How does the game end?

The current implementation completes when the target penalty threshold is reached.
