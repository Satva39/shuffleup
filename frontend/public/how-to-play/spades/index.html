How to Play Spades Online

Spades is a four-player partnership trick-taking game. North/South form one team and East/West form the other. Spades are always trump in this ShuffleUp implementation.

Objective

Make your team’s combined bid while taking tricks, and avoid accumulating too many bags or missing the contract.

How to play Spades: step by step

  1. The server deals a standard deck so each player has 13 cards. Each player submits a bid for expected tricks.
  2. Players take turns playing cards to 13 tricks. Follow the led suit when possible.
  3. Spades are trump. They generally cannot be led until broken unless the player has only spades, as defined by the game rules.
  4. At the end of the hand, the server compares the team’s tricks with its bid. A made non-Nil contract scores 10 times the bid plus overtrick bags; a failed contract loses 10 times the bid.
  5. Bags accumulate between hands. Every 10 bags adds a 100-point penalty. Nil bids are scored separately. The first team reaching the configured 500-point target wins.

Tips for a new player

  • Bid realistically based on your hand rather than aiming for the highest number.
  • Track whether spades have been broken before leading them.
  • Watch your team’s bag total; small overtricks can matter later.

Frequently asked questions

How many players can play Spades?

Exactly four players, split into two teams.

What is trump in Spades?

Spades are always the trump suit in this implementation.

Rules note: This guide follows the version currently implemented on ShuffleUp. Some traditional card games have regional variants, and local rules may differ. The game result and valid actions inside ShuffleUp are determined by its game server.
