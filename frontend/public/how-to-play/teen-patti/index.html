How to Play Teen Patti Online

ShuffleUp’s Teen Patti is a three-card comparison game played over a series of rounds. This implementation uses an in-game points system; it does not include betting, chips or real-money wagering.

Objective

Win rounds by having the strongest eligible three-card hand or by remaining the only active player. Round wins add to your overall points.

How to play Teen Patti: step by step

  1. The server deals three cards to each player from a standard 52-card deck. Opponents’ private cards remain hidden.
  2. During your turn, choose an available action: play or fold. A folded player is out of the current round.
  3. If only one active player remains, that player wins the round. Otherwise, the server resolves the remaining hands in a showdown when required.
  4. The implemented hand order from weakest to strongest is High Card, Pair, Color, Sequence, Pure Sequence and Trail.
  5. A round winner receives one point. The match consists of 11 rounds, and the highest round-win score takes the result; the implemented seat order breaks a tie.

Tips for a new player

  • Learn the hand order before deciding whether to stay in a round.
  • Pay attention to whether opponents have folded or are still active.
  • Use the visible points system to track round progress; this version has no betting or currency.

Frequently asked questions

Does ShuffleUp Teen Patti use real money?

No. The current ShuffleUp implementation is a points-only card game without betting or currency.

How many cards are dealt?

Each player receives three cards.
