How to Play Twenty-Nine (29) Online

Twenty-Nine, also called the 29 card game, is a four-player partnership trick-taking game. ShuffleUp uses a 32-card deck and a bidding phase to set a target for the contract team.

Objective

Win the bidding contract and capture enough card points as a team to meet the bid.

How to play Twenty-Nine (29): step by step

  1. Four players sit in two partnerships: North/South and East/West. The 32-card deck uses J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8 and 7 in each suit.
  2. An initial four-card deal is followed by an auction. Bids use the supported range of 16 to 28.
  3. The winning bidder selects a hidden trump suit. The remaining cards are dealt before trick play begins.
  4. Players must follow the led suit when possible. Once trump is revealed, trump cards beat non-trump cards. The trick winner leads next.
  5. Card points are J=3, 9=2, A=1 and 10=1. The bidding team’s total is compared with its contract. A made hand gives the team one game point, a failed contract gives the bidding team minus one, and the last trick adds one point to the hand total. The match target is six game points.

Tips for a new player

  • Prioritize the high-value Jacks and Nines when estimating your trick strength.
  • Remember that the trump suit can remain hidden until the game reveals it.
  • Keep track of the contract and card points captured by your partnership.

Frequently asked questions

How many players does 29 support?

Exactly four players in two partnerships.

Which cards carry points?

Jacks are worth 3, Nines 2, Aces 1 and Tens 1 in this implementation.
