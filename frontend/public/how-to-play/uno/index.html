How to Play UNO Online

UNO is a shedding card game built around matching the active color or number and using action cards at the right moment. ShuffleUp’s implementation supports 2–12 players and carries round scores into a longer match.

Objective

Be the first player to empty your hand in a round, then accumulate round points toward the match target.

How to play UNO: step by step

  1. Each player receives seven cards. A valid non-wild card begins the discard pile.
  2. On your turn, play a legal card that matches the active color or the top card’s number, or use a special card when its rules allow it.
  3. If you cannot or choose not to make a legal play under the game’s available actions, draw a card. A Wild may require you to choose the next color.
  4. Special cards change the flow of play according to their type, including effects such as skip, reverse and draw penalties.
  5. When a player empties their hand, the server resolves the round and adds the appropriate round score. The match target is 500 points.

Tips for a new player

  • Keep an eye on the active color before choosing a card.
  • Use wild and action cards thoughtfully rather than spending them automatically.
  • Watch how many cards other players hold so you can plan for the next turns.

Frequently asked questions

How many cards do players start with?

Each player receives seven cards.

What is the match target?

The current ShuffleUp implementation ends the match when a player reaches at least 500 points.

Rules note: This guide follows the version currently implemented on ShuffleUp. Some traditional card games have regional variants, and local rules may differ. The game result and valid actions inside ShuffleUp are determined by its game server.
