How to Play War Online

War is a two-player card game built around repeated rank comparisons. Both players reveal a card, and the higher rank wins the battle pile. Ties trigger an additional War resolution.

Objective

Collect the available cards and be the last player with cards remaining.

How to play War: step by step

  1. The server shuffles a standard 52-card deck and deals 26 cards to each player.
  2. When a battle begins, each active player reveals one card face-up.
  3. The higher rank wins the battle pile and takes the cards. Aces are high, and suits do not break ties.
  4. If revealed ranks tie, the game enters War resolution. Tied players place a face-down card when available, followed by another face-up comparison card.
  5. Further ties repeat the War procedure until the pile is resolved. A player with no cards is eliminated, and the last player with cards wins when the game reaches its completion state.

Tips for a new player

  • Know the rank order: Aces are high and suits do not matter.
  • A War can create a larger pile, so the next comparison may transfer many cards at once.
  • The server controls reveals and card transfers; wait for the battle result before the next move.

Frequently asked questions

How many players does War support?

Exactly two real players.

Do suits matter?

No. Rank decides the comparison, and suits do not break ties.
